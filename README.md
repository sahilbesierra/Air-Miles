# ✈️ Air Miles — Personal Flight & Travel Logbook

**Your flights. Your miles. Your journey.**

Air Miles is a digital travel logbook designed to help users record their flight details, track accumulated air miles, and maintain a personal history of their air travel — all in one place.

Instead of relying on scattered booking emails, tickets, or manual records, Air Miles provides a centralized platform to preserve flight information and keep track of every journey.

---

## 📌 About the Project

Air Miles aims to simplify the way travelers manage and organize their flight history.

Users can add their flight details, maintain a record of their journeys, and monitor the total miles accumulated through air travel.

The project is designed with future expansion in mind, allowing additional travel-related features, analytics, and visualizations to be integrated over time.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🛫 Flight Management

* Add and maintain personal flight records.
* Store airline and flight information.
* Record departure and destination airports.
* Save travel dates and other journey details.
* Maintain a centralized history of flights.

### 📍 Mileage Tracking

* Track accumulated flight miles.
* Maintain a record of distances traveled.
* View mileage information across recorded journeys.

### 📖 Digital Travel Logbook

* Preserve flight information in one place.
* Organize past journeys.
* Maintain a personal digital record of air travel.

### 🚀 Future Enhancements

* Interactive travel dashboard.
* Flight route visualization on a world map.
* Airline-wise mileage analytics.
* Travel statistics and insights.
* Boarding pass and ticket attachments.
* Travel history timeline.
* Enhanced search and filtering.
* Additional personalized travel features.
